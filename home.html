08 questions

questions.

do i need to sign anything?

no. sh1ft never asks your wallet to sign. you hold, and SOL arrives. connecting a wallet on the site only reads its address.

where does payroll come from?

$SH1FT's creator fees. pump.fun's fee sharing sends them to the payroll wallet, and that wallet only pays wages.

who holds the payroll key?

sh1ft's server, derived from one secret. the code sends payroll in one way only: to the wallets on the last shift's roster, split by bag.

what if nobody runs payroll?

it runs by itself once a day, and anyone can press it any time. a shift's fees roll into the next payroll if it's missed.

does buying more count?

yes, from the next shift. inside a shift your wage uses the bag you clocked in with. buying never fires you; selling or sending does.
